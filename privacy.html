Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Autotube Engine ("the app") is a single-operator automation tool. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

The app requests one Google OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. This permits uploading video files to YouTube channels owned by the operator who authorizes the app. The app does not read, list, modify, or delete any other YouTube data, and does not access Google account profile information beyond what is required to complete the OAuth sign-in.

How the data is used

The OAuth credential is used exclusively to upload finished video files to the operator's own YouTube channels (@SecurityBytesDaily, @TerminalMastery). It is not used for any other purpose.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the operator's own private server, used to refresh access for subsequent uploads, and is never transmitted to any third party. It can be revoked at any time from the operator's Google Account permissions page.

Sharing

No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data from other sources

The app also reads publicly available RSS news feeds and public stock-footage APIs. These contain no personal data and are unrelated to any Google user.
